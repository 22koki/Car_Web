## Display guidance

- Retain a visible “Photo credits” link to this document or equivalent credits in the deployed site.
- `hero.webp` depicts a **Porsche Panamera Turbo**, not a Porsche 911. Use it with general brand copy or an accurate caption.
- `detail.webp` is a separate green GT3 photograph. Do not present it as another angle of the black GT3 listing.
- Inventory galleries deliberately use one verified representative photo per sample car; add dealer-owned matching vehicle photographs when real stock is introduced.
- The road photograph is in Alberta, Canada; do not label it as a Kenyan road.

## Animated concept vehicle

`concept-car.webp` is an AI-generated fictional silver concept sports car created for the animated driving scene. It is a decorative illustration, not a listing photograph or a real vehicle offered for sale.

A deployed attribution page is available at `/credits.html`. Layouts may crop the display of the resized WebP photographs; image licenses and creator attributions remain unchanged.


### Concept-car generation brief

Built-in image generation produced the transparent fictional silver sports coupe in `public/images/concept-car.webp`. The project copy was encoded as WebP with its alpha channel preserved. The original generated PNG is not needed by the application.

Prompt: One photorealistic premium unbranded sports coupe in a perfectly horizontal side profile, facing right; pearl silver metallic body, lime brake calipers, black glass roof and dark alloy wheels. Full car visible on a genuinely transparent background, clean studio product lighting, no environment, text, manufacturer logos or watermarks. Fictional concept for the animated scene, not a photographed inventory vehicle.
