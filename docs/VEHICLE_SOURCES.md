# Demonstration inventory and specification references

This is a design and functionality demonstration, not a verified vehicle marketplace. Asking prices in KES, registration years, mileage, colours, badges and individual vehicle descriptions are sample inventory. They are not current market valuations or confirmed dealer stock. Reference photographs may show a different trim or model year. Replace every record and photograph with authorised, verified inventory before using the site to advertise actual cars.

Manufacturer reference pages below were checked on 3 November 2026. Specifications are tied to the stated reference variant, not automatically to the latest model. Equipment varies by market. Power uses metric horsepower (PS); electric-car temporary launch boost is disclosed separately. Acceleration means 0–100 km/h, typically under manufacturer test conditions and with Launch Control where available. Default/limited top speeds are used instead of optional higher limits.

## Finance illustration

`calculateFinance` estimates a fixed-rate amortising loan with equal monthly payments. It assumes monthly interest at the supplied annual percentage divided by twelve, with the deposit paid separately. It excludes arrangement fees, balloon payments, insurance, taxes, changes in interest rates and lender-specific charges. It is not a credit offer, finance approval or affordability assessment. The default rate selected by the interface is an editable example, not a researched Kenyan lender quotation.
